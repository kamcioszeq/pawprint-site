Privacy Policy

PawPrint (Łapka) app · version of 1 November 2026

1. In short

  • You do not create an account with an e-mail or password. The app makes a random account number itself.
  • Prints, photos and pet details stay on your phone.
  • Only what credits, purchases and AI styles need reaches our server — and telemetry, if you agree to it.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps, and we do not sell data.
  • You can delete your data from the server yourself: Settings → “Delete my data from the server”.

2. Data that stays on your phone

Paw prints (a record of the screen touches), photos of paws and pets, pet details (name, species, date of birth, breed, note), AI style pictures, settings and a local log of technical events. We do not send these to us. They may be part of your phone's backup (iCloud or a computer), as you have set it. The account number and your tier are kept in the Keychain on this device only.

4. AI styles and Google Gemini

When you pick an AI style and tap “Generate”, after your separate consent the print's image (a grey touch map, and for “On photo” a part of the chosen photo) goes through our server to the Google Gemini API (Google LLC), which makes the picture. Google may process this data in the USA, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. Google's terms: ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. You can withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy; without it the app works, except for AI styles.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Google Cloud (Google Ireland Limited) — server, database and files, region europe-west1 (Belgium). Technical logs may also be stored in Google's infrastructure outside the EEA.
  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC) — AI styles, only with your consent.
  • Apple — App Store payments and notifications. We never see your card details.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Account, credits, purchases and generation — performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Sending the image to Google Gemini and telemetry — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Security, limits and abuse prevention, handling reports — our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

7. Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account → “Delete my data from the server”. We delete the account, balance, tiers given by hand, generation jobs with their pictures, bug reports and wishes, and the notification token. What stays, without the account number: purchase records (so “Restore purchases” brings Plus and PRO back), the credit history for accounting, campaign rewards taken, and the date of deletion under a hash of the number — so starting credits and rewards are not given twice. Data on the phone goes when you delete prints, pets or the app.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict its processing, to data portability, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). Write to lapka.support@gmail.com with the account number from Settings → Account. You may complain to the Polish data protection authority, UODO (uodo.gov.pl), or the authority where you live.

9. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13. Consent to AI styles may be given by a person aged 16 or over, or their guardian.

10. Changes

We will tell you about changes to this policy in the app and ask you to read it again.
