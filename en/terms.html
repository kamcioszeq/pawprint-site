2. What the app is

PawPrint records your pet's paw pressed to the phone's screen or taken from a photo, styles it, and prepares an engraving, a tattoo, a passport and a card to share. AI styles are made by an outside service, Google Gemini; the result is generated automatically and may differ from what you expect.

3. Account

The app makes a random account tied to the phone itself — no e-mail or password. You find the account number in Settings → Account; give it when you ask about purchases or need help.

4. Plus, PRO and credits

  • Credits are spent on generating AI styles; one generation costs the credits the app shows. More credits come in packs.
  • Credits are not money: they cannot be exchanged for money or moved to another account.
  • When a generation fails, its credits come back.

  • To protect the service the number of generations is limited; with many requests at once, generations wait in a queue.
