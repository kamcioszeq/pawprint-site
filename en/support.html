Common questions

I lost Plus/PRO after changing phones.

A generation failed — what about my credits?
They come back automatically. Tap the coins in the editor to see your credit history.

A generation is “waiting in the queue”.
With many requests at once, generations start one after another. They will run by themselves — nothing to do.

How do I delete my data?
